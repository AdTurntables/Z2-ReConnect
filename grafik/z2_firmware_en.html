Z² RE:CONNECT · FIRMWARE FOR TRAKTOR KONTROL Z2

Your Z2 on the iPad.
No drivers.

Z² Re:Connect makes the Z2 class compliant: audio and MIDI work straight on iPad and iPhone, e.g. with djay Pro, 4 decks and DVS.

♪Built-in USB MIDI

All 48 buttons, 26 knobs and faders and 3 encoders send MIDI. On connect, every knob and fader position is sent once, so the app starts in sync.

▦4 pad layers per deck

Switch with DECK A / C (right side B / D). Each layer sends on its own MIDI channel and has its own colour. The firmware remembers every layer, so the pads show the right state the moment you switch.

⇄Nothing lost

Audio, the built-in mixer, timecode via the TRAKTOR buttons and Traktor on your computer work just like before.
