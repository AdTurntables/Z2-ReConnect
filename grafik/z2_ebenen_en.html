Four layers per pad row

DECK A and DECK C switch the left pads, DECK B and DECK D the right ones. Each button turns its light on or off. The two DECK LEDs show which layer is active.

How it works

  • No DECK LED lit → layer 1
  • Only DECK A lit → layer 2
  • Only DECK C lit → layer 3
  • A and C lit → layer 4
  • SHIFT + pad deletes the cue
  • Same on the right with DECK B / D

New in Beta 2

  • Cue colours: the pads show the colour the cue has in djay
  • Level meters A / B follow the level of deck 1 / 2
  • Unplug and replug USB: the LEDs come straight back
Under the hood: each layer sends on its own MIDI channel (1, 4, 5, 6). The firmware remembers the LEDs of every layer, so the pads are right the moment you switch.
